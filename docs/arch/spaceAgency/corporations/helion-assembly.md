## Ability

Passive Ability: When you research a tech, you may mark the matching reward space on this organization and gain its reward. Probe/orange tech grants 1 energy, scan/purple tech grants 1 signal token, and computer/blue tech grants 1 data.

Free Action Ability (Once per round): turn off one of your probe or scan techs and tuck one card from your hand for income, then clear all reward spaces marked on this organization. The turned-off tech still counts as researched but its ability can no longer be used.
